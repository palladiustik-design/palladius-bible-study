Privacy Policy — Palladius Bible Study

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Palladius Bible Study (“the App”) is a Bible study application for the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church tradition. Your privacy matters to us. The App is designed to run on your device, and the developer does not operate any server and does not collect, store, or receive any of your personal data.

Information We Collect

The developer of the App does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any personal information. The App has no user accounts and does not require you to sign in. All Scripture text, commentaries, dictionaries, Strongʼs data, and study tools are stored locally on your device and work fully offline. The one exception is the optional AI verse analysis feature described below, which you choose to enable and which sends requests directly from your device to Google using your own key — never to the developer.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes, app settings, and (if you choose to use AI analysis) your Google Gemini API key are saved only on your device, in the App’s local storage. This information never leaves your device except as you direct (see below) and is removed if you delete the App.

Optional AI Verse Analysis (Google Gemini)

The App includes an optional feature that can generate a deeper explanation or analysis of a Bible verse using Google’s Gemini AI. This feature is off unless you use it, and it works as follows:

  • To use it, you provide your own personal Google Gemini API key, which is stored only on your device. The developer never receives this key.
  • When you request an analysis, the App sends the relevant verse or passage text and your request directly from your device to Google’s Gemini servers, authenticated with your own API key.
  • Google processes that request and returns the explanation to your device. This processing is performed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy and the terms governing your Google/Gemini API key. The verse-analysis “brain” is the App itself; Google is used only to generate the text of the explanation.
  • The developer of the App operates no server in this process and does not receive, store, log, or have access to your requests, the responses, your API key, or any related content. Nothing is sent to the developer.

If you never use the AI analysis feature, the App makes no network requests and functions entirely offline.

Licensing / Device Activation

To manage access, the App may generate a random, anonymous device identifier stored securely on your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any Apple ID, is not transmitted to the developer, and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Third Parties & Tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The developer does not share any information with third parties, because the developer does not collect any. The only third party the App can contact is Google’s Gemini API, and only when you choose to use the optional AI analysis feature with your own API key, as described above.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
