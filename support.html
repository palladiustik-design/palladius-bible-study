Palladius Bible Study — Support

Thank you for using Palladius Bible Study, an Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Bible study app with Scripture in Amharic, Geʽez, Tigrinya, Afaan Oromo and English (KJV), plus commentaries, a dictionary, Strongʼs numbers, and cross-references.

Contact

For help, questions, or to report a problem, email palladiustik@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version so we can help faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. All content is stored on your device and works fully offline.
What is the activation / unlock screen?
The app shows a device code and asks for an unlock key. Contact us at the email above with your device code to receive a key.
How do I switch languages?
Use the language selector in the reader to switch between Amharic, Geʽez, Tigrinya, Afaan Oromo, and English, or view translations side by side.
How do I bookmark or highlight a verse?
Tap and hold a verse to highlight it or add a bookmark or note. Your bookmarks and notes are saved on your device.
My Ethiopic text looks wrong or boxes appear.
The app bundles Noto Sans/Serif Ethiopic fonts and should render correctly on iOS 13 and later. If you see issues, email us with a screenshot.
